         QUICK START GUIDE

STEP 1: OPEN THE APP
Double-click "CoralDetector.exe"

STEP 2: SELECT PHOTO
Click "Select Coral Image" button
Find your coral photo
Click "Open"

STEP 3: SEE RESULT

✅ HEALTHY CORAL = No bleaching
⚠️ BLEACHED DETECTED = Bleaching found

STEP 4: CHECK CONFIDENCE
90-100% = Very sure
70-89% = Moderately sure
Below 70% = Try clearer photo
