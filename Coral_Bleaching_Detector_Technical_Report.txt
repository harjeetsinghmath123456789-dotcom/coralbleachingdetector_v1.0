## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Coral Bleaching Detector achieves **93.7% accuracy** in identifying coral bleaching from underwater photographs, with an **AUC score of 98.8%** demonstrating excellent discrimination between healthy and bleached coral.

| Key Metric | Score |
|------------|-------|
| Overall Accuracy | 93.7% |
| AUC Score | 98.8% |
| F1 Score | 93.7% |
| Testing Samples | 287 images |

---

## METHODOLOGY

### Model Architecture
- **Feature Extractor:** SqueezeNet (1000 features)
- **Classifier:** Neural Network (100, 50 hidden layers)
- **Training Data:** Coral reef photographs
- **Validation Method:** Cross-validation

### Testing Protocol
- Total test images: 287
- Healthy coral samples: 143
- Bleached coral samples: 144
- Test environment: Windows 10/11

## INTERPRETATION

### What These Numbers Mean

**93.7% Accuracy**
- Out of 100 coral photos, the system correctly identifies ~94
- Only ~6 images may require human verification

**98.8% AUC**
- Excellent ability to distinguish healthy from bleached coral
- Ranks in top tier for image classification systems

**False Positive Rate:** 6.3%
- Occasionally marks healthy coral as bleached

**False Negative Rate:** 6.2%  
- Occasionally misses bleached coral
